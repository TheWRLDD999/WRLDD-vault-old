His Life

Jarad Anthony Higgins

Juice WRLD was an American rapper, singer and songwriter known for blending hip-hop, emo rap and melodic songwriting. Born on December 2, 1998, in Chicago, Illinois, he rose to international recognition with songs including “Lucid Dreams” and “All Girls Are the Same”. His music often explored emotion, relationships and personal struggles. He died in December 2019, aged 21. His music and influence continue to reach listeners around the world.
